🏠 Pocket Estimate

AI-powered property damage assessment — scan, document, and estimate in minutes, not hours

Pocket Estimate combines Apple LiDAR room scanning, photo documentation, voice dictation, and AI analysis to produce professional-grade damage exposure ranges and itemized estimates. Built by a 35+ year insurance industry professional.

  • 📡 LiDAR room scanning for precise spatial data
  • 📸 Up to 10 annotated photos per room
  • 🎙️ Voice dictation for hands-free scope entry
  • 🤖 AI reviews images, scans, and notes together
  • 📊 Low-to-high damage exposure range
  • 🧾 Itemized estimates by room and phase
  • 📄 Export to PDF or XLS and share instantly
  • 🏢 Supports dwelling, condo, and commercial properties

Designed for insurance adjusters, contractors, and property owners. Calibrated to real field costs — not generic databases.
