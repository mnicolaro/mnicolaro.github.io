StorySheet Support

Welcome to StorySheet support. StorySheet helps you turn photos, short videos, captions, backgrounds, and layouts into polished visual stories you can save, export, print, and share.

Common StorySheet Features

  • Select and arrange photos and short videos
  • Add captions, notes, and descriptive text
  • Choose layouts and backgrounds
  • Use markup tools for emphasis and annotation
  • Create PDFs and other supported exports
  • Print, email, save, or share your finished StorySheet
  • Use supported social-sharing options when available
